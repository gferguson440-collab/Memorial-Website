His Journey

A Life Remembered

Mr. Harrison Wright, lovingly known to many as “Dutty Harry,” was a man whose life was shaped by hard work, family, friendship, and love.

Born to Louanna Gillett on September 8, 1968, Harrison attended Grace Primary School and later went on to Sec Two.

His working life began at Caye Chapel, where he worked as a bartender. Later, he became known for his skills as a mechanic, working on golf carts, boats, motorcycles, engines, and whatever else needed fixing.

Harrison was more than a mechanic. He was someone people could turn to. He had a way of fixing not only machines, but situations and problems as well.

He met Abby Williams, and they were married on November 6, 1996. Together they built a life centered around family before moving to San Pedro and beginning their family.

His greatest blessings were his seven children and ten grandchildren, whom he cherished deeply.
