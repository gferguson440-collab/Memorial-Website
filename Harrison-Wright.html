His Story

Harrison Wright, lovingly known to many as “Dutty Harry,” was born on September 8, 1968.

He was the son of Louanna Gillett and the grandson of Hortence Gillett. He attended Grace Primary School and later continued his education at Sec Two.

Harrison was a hardworking man who could turn his hands to almost anything. He worked as a bartender at Caye Chapel before eventually beginning a new chapter of his life in San Pedro.

Harrison had a natural talent for fixing things. He worked on golf carts, boats, motorcycles and engines, and he was always willing to help someone solve a problem.

His life was filled with family, friendship, laughter and memories that will remain with the people who knew and loved him.

His Life

A Man Who Could Fix Almost Anything

Harrison had a special ability to understand machines and figure out how things worked.

Golf carts, boats, motorcycles and engines were among the many things he worked on. Whenever someone needed help solving a problem, Harrison was often the person they could turn to.

His willingness to help others was one of the qualities that made him special to his family, friends and community.

Memories

Oreo

Harrison's beloved dog, Oreo, was also an important part of his life. The memories shared with Oreo will remain part of the story of a man who loved deeply and cared for those around him.

Service & Friendship

A Helping Hand

Harrison was known for helping people whenever he could. Whether it involved repairing an engine, fixing a golf cart, working on a boat or simply helping someone solve a problem, he was willing to lend a hand.

Special memories are shared with Jean Villatoro, George Ferguson, Alfonso Tosta and Ethel Villatoro, along with the many friends and family members whose lives he touched.

A Part of His Story

In 2011, Harrison changed his surname from Gillett to Wright through deed poll. His family remembers the questions and conversations surrounding the different surnames carried by members of the family.

It became another part of Harrison's unique story — a story that will continue to be remembered by those who knew him.

His Legacy

Harrison will be remembered for his kindness, wisdom, hard work, loyalty, generosity and love for his family and friends.

His laughter, stories, friendship and willingness to help others created memories that will remain long after his passing.

Though he is no longer here in person, the impact he made on the lives around him will continue.
