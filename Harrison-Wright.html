Mr. Harrison Wright

Forever Remembered

Life Story

Remembering the life and legacy of Mr. Harrison Wright.

Harrison Wright, lovingly known by many as “Dutty Harry”, was a cherished father, grandfather, brother, friend, mechanic, and beloved member of his community.

Born on September 8, 1968, Harrison grew up with strong family roots. He attended Grace Primary School and later Sec Two before beginning his working life.

He worked as a bartender at Caye Chapel, where he met Abby Williams. They were married on November 6, 1996, and later made their home in San Pedro, where they began raising their family.

Harrison was known for being someone who could fix almost anything. He worked on golf carts, boats, motorcycles, and engines, and was always willing to help someone solve a problem.

His kindness, loyalty, hard work, humor, and love for his family created memories that will remain with everyone who knew him.
