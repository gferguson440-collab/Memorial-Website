HIS STORY

A Life Remembered

Remembering Harrison Wright and the beautiful memories he leaves behind.

Today, we gather to remember and honor Mr. Harrison Wright, better known to many of us as “Dutty Harry.”

It is difficult to find the words to capture a life as fully and as generously as he lived. He touched so many of us in ways we probably never fully realize.

But his legacy is clear: through his kindness, his wisdom, his laughter, his friendship, and the love he gave so freely, his memory will continue to live on.

Harrison was a person whose presence could make a difference. The memories shared by family and friends will remain a treasured part of the story of his life.
