A Place to Remember

Every life has a story worth remembering.

Forever Remembered creates personal digital memorials that give families a lasting place to honor and celebrate the lives of their loved ones.

Each memorial can include photographs, a life story, important moments, memories, and messages from family and friends.

How It Works

Creating a meaningful digital memorial is simple.

1

Share Their Story

Provide us with photographs, information, memories, and important details about your loved one.

2

We Create the Memorial

We create a beautiful personal digital memorial dedicated to their life and legacy.

3

Receive Your QR Code

Each memorial receives its own unique QR code that connects directly to the memorial.

4

Remember Anytime

Family and friends can scan the QR code with a phone and visit the memorial whenever they wish.

Our Purpose

Losing someone we love can leave behind countless memories, stories, photographs, and moments that deserve to be preserved.

Our goal is to provide families with a beautiful digital space where those memories can be kept together and shared with the people who matter most.

Because while a person may no longer be with us, the memories they created can continue to live on.

Your Memorial. Your Choice.

Families should have control over how their loved one's memorial is shared.

Private Memorials
